About Smart Zambia

Building a smarter future for Zambia, one project at a time.

Who We Are

Smart Zambia is a home‑grown engineering firm that brings together expertise in Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, and IT. We are passionate about delivering affordable, fast, and reliable solutions for homes, businesses, and national infrastructure.

Founded in 2026, our team of certified engineers and technicians works across the country, from Lusaka to the Copperbelt, to make smart living a reality for every Zambian.
