About Smart Zambia

We are a team of engineers, innovators, and problem‑solvers dedicated to building a smarter, more sustainable Zambia.

Our Story

Building Zambia's Future

Smart Zambia was founded with a clear vision: to deliver world‑class engineering solutions that are accessible, reliable, and tailored to the unique needs of Zambian communities. From humble beginnings, we have grown into a trusted partner for residential, commercial, and industrial projects across the country.

Our strength lies in our local expertise combined with a global mindset. We don't just build structures — we build relationships, empower communities, and drive innovation at every level.

Our Journey

The Timeline

Key milestones that have shaped Smart Zambia into what it is today.

2010

Foundation

Smart Zambia was established in Lusaka with a small team of 5 engineers, focusing on residential electrical installations.

2014

Expansion into Civil & Mechanical

We broadened our services to include civil construction and mechanical systems, taking on larger commercial projects.

2018

Smart Home & IT Division

Launched our IT and smart home automation division, integrating cutting‑edge technology into everyday living.

2023

Nationwide Reach

With projects in Lusaka, Kitwe, Ndola, and beyond, we became a trusted name across all 10 provinces of Zambia.

Today

Building the Future

We continue to innovate, train local talent, and deliver sustainable engineering solutions for generations to come.
